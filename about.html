Follow Us On

UNEDITS is a boutique creative studio built for brands that refuse to blend in. We work across UI/UX design, graphic design, branding, video editing, and website design — turning ideas into experiences people actually notice.

Founded by Nitin and Amit, UNEDITS was born from a simple belief: good design isn't decoration, it's strategy. Every project we take on is treated like a partnership, not a transaction — we dig into what makes a brand different, then build a visual identity and digital presence around that difference.

We're not a freelance gig or a one-person shop. UNEDITS is a studio built for the long run — working with startups, growing businesses, and premium brands who want work that holds up next to the best in the world.
