About Us

UNEDITS is a digital technology studio specializing in website development, UI/UX design, graphic design, branding, video editing, and social media management. We design with intention, build with precision, and create digital experiences that help ambitious brands stand apart.

Every project starts with understanding what actually makes a brand different, then engineering a digital presence around that difference — not decoration, strategy. We treat each engagement as a partnership, not a one-off transaction.

UNEDITS is headquartered in Rohtak, Haryana, India, working with clients across India and internationally.
