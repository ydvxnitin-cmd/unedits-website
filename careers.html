Follow Us On

UNEDITS is a boutique multidisciplinary creative studio specializing in video editing, graphic design, branding, UI/UX, and website design. We're always looking for people who take their craft seriously and care about the details that separate good work from great work. Working here means owning your output — whether that's a single video edit or a full brand identity. We're a small, close-knit team, which means your work is visible and your impact is real.

Open Positions

We don't have an active opening right now, but we're always on the lookout for talented editors, designers, and creatives. If you think you'd be a good fit, send us your portfolio — we'd love to see your work.

Why Join Us

You get real creative freedom here — no watered-down briefs, no layers of sign-off before an idea sees daylight. Every project you work on is a live client engagement, not a training exercise, so your work goes out into the world and holds up. Being a small team means a flexible working style and the room to experiment with new tools, techniques, and formats instead of sticking to one fixed playbook.

How to Apply

Send us your portfolio and a short note about yourself at careers@unedits.in. Shortlisted candidates will hear back within 5–7 days.
