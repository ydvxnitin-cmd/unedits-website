Follow Us On

At UNEDITS ("we," "our," "us"), we understand that trust is the foundation of any good working relationship, and that includes how we handle the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy is designed to give you a clear and complete picture of what information we collect through our website (unedits.in) and our client engagement process, why we collect it, how we use it, and what choices you have regarding your own data. By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

When you visit our website or reach out to us, we may collect several categories of information, depending on how you interact with us.

Contact Information: When you fill out our contact form, inquiry form, or careers application, we collect details such as your name, email address, phone number, and any company or organization you represent. This allows us to respond to you appropriately and keep track of ongoing conversations.

Project and Brief Information: Once you engage us for a project, you may share detailed information about your brand, business goals, creative preferences, reference materials, and other project-specific content. This information is essential for us to deliver work that actually meets your needs, and it is treated as part of our confidential client relationship.

Usage and Technical Data: Like most websites, ours automatically collects certain technical information when you browse — this includes your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring website. We gather this through tools like Google Analytics or similar services, and it helps us understand how visitors use our website so we can continue improving it.

Cookies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. More detail on this is available in our separate Cookie Policy.

How We Use Your Information

We collect and use your information for a range of legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries, questions, or requests you submit through our website
  • Communicating with you throughout a project — from initial brief to final delivery
  • Preparing proposals, agreements, and invoices related to your project
  • Improving our website's design, content, and overall user experience based on usage patterns
  • Sending occasional updates about our services, portfolio, or availability, but only if you've opted in to receive such communication
  • Complying with any legal obligations that may apply to us as a business

We do not use your personal information for any purpose beyond what is reasonably necessary to operate our business and serve our clients well.

Sharing of Information

We want to be upfront: we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. However, in the course of running our business, we may share limited information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate — for example, form providers like Formspree that process contact submissions, email hosting providers like Google Workspace, analytics services, or payment processing tools. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their specific function and are expected to handle it responsibly.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy — for instance, we may retain project-related communication for the duration of our working relationship and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping purposes. If you'd like your information removed sooner, you're welcome to reach out to us directly.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. That said, no method of data transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access the data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, request deletion of your data, or withdraw consent for us to contact you. If you'd like to exercise any of these rights, simply reach out to us and we'll respond as promptly as we can.

Changes to This Policy

As our business grows and evolves, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new practices or legal requirements. We'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page whenever changes are made, and we encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us at help@unedits.in. We're happy to address any concerns directly.
