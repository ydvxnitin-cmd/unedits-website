Privacy Policy

At UNEDITS ("we," "our," "us"), we understand that trust is the foundation of any good working relationship, and that includes how we handle the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website (unedits.in) and our client engagement process, why we collect it, how we use it, and what choices you have regarding your own data. By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

When you visit our website or reach out to us, we may collect several categories of information, depending on how you interact with us.

Contact Information: When you fill out our contact form, inquiry form, or careers application, we collect details such as your name, email address, phone number, and any company or organization you represent.

Project and Brief Information: Once you engage us for a project, you may share detailed information about your brand, business goals, creative preferences, and reference materials, treated as part of our confidential client relationship.

Usage and Technical Data: Like most websites, ours automatically collects certain technical information when you browse — including IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring website.

Cookies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • Responding to inquiries, questions, or requests submitted through our website
  • Communicating with you throughout a project — from initial brief to final delivery
  • Preparing proposals, agreements, and invoices related to your project
  • Improving our website's design, content, and overall user experience
  • Sending occasional updates about our services or availability, only if you've opted in
  • Complying with any legal obligations that may apply to us as a business

We do not use your personal information for any purpose beyond what is reasonably necessary to operate our business and serve our clients well.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share limited information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate — such as form providers, email hosting, and analytics services — who only receive what's necessary to perform their function.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. If you'd like your information removed sooner, you're welcome to reach out to us directly.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data, or withdraw consent for us to contact you. Reach out and we'll respond as promptly as we can.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new practices or legal requirements. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at help@unedits.in.
