Follow Us On

At UNEDITS, we're committed to delivering creative work that our clients are genuinely happy with, and we believe that clear expectations around payments, cancellations, and refunds help build a healthier working relationship from the very start. This policy explains how we handle these situations across our video editing, graphic design, branding, UI/UX, and website design projects.

Advance Payments

For nearly all projects, we require an advance payment before any work begins. This advance secures your place in our project schedule, allows us to allocate time and resources specifically to your project, and covers early-stage costs such as research, planning, and initial creative development. Because this advance represents a real commitment of our time and capacity — time that could otherwise be allocated to another client — advance payments are non-refundable once a project has been confirmed and scheduled, even if you later decide not to proceed.

Cancellations Initiated by the Client

We understand that circumstances change, and sometimes a client may need to pause or cancel a project after work has already started. In such cases, you will be billed for all work completed up to the point of cancellation, calculated on a fair, pro-rata basis relative to the total project scope and fee. Any portion of the advance payment that exceeds the value of work already completed will not be refunded, as it reflects the time and resources already committed to your project. We encourage clients to communicate as early as possible if they're considering cancellation, so we can discuss options and minimize any unnecessary costs on either side.

Cancellations Initiated by UNEDITS

While rare, there may be situations where we are unable to complete a project — due to unforeseen circumstances, capacity issues, or a mutual decision that the engagement isn't the right fit. In such cases, we will refund any payments received for work that has not yet been delivered or completed, ensuring you're only charged for the value you've actually received.

Mid-Project Changes in Scope

If, during the course of a project, the client requests significant changes to the original scope — such as an entirely new creative direction after a milestone has already been approved — this may be treated as a new phase of work rather than a revision, and additional charges may apply. We will always communicate any such changes and associated costs clearly before proceeding, so there are no surprises.

Revisions Are Not the Same as Refunds

Every project includes a defined number of revision rounds, as specified in your project agreement, giving you the opportunity to guide the creative direction and request changes based on your feedback. Because creative work inherently involves some degree of subjective preference, refunds are not offered simply because a client's taste evolves or changes after milestones have already been reviewed and approved. Our revision process exists specifically to address this and ensure the final result aligns with what you're looking for, within the agreed scope.

Delays Caused by the Client

If a project is delayed due to the client's own delay in providing feedback, assets, approvals, or other required input, this does not entitle the client to a refund or discount, as our team's time and schedule have already been allocated for the project regardless of these delays.

How to Raise a Concern

If you believe there has been an error in billing, or you'd like to discuss a cancellation or potential refund, we encourage you to reach out to us directly at help@unedits.in. We genuinely want to resolve any concerns fairly, and most issues can be worked out through a straightforward conversation.
